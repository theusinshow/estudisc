# Compact IFSC rules

The pinned curriculum requirements and Concept mappings are authoritative for intent. Historical Integrated exams inform reasoning style, contextualization, stimuli, distractors and approximate difficulty; they never remove official requirements.

Preserve target-edition mapping uncertainty. Existing historical mapping is not proof that the current target edital was verified. Inventory-only Concepts/lessons are not teaching coverage. Keep missing material visible in notes and export.

Use appropriate IFSC-style transfer alongside foundation/direct practice. Generated questions are generated, human-authored questions human_created, legitimately inspired questions derived. Never label invented questions official_exam. Do not copy protected/annulled/reserved official questions or assets into lessons, prompts, public directories or generated exports. Catalog historical metadata intentionally excludes protected question text/assets.

Do not alter core curriculum, scores, planner/mastery policy or reservation behavior while authoring. A missing interaction or official mapping correction is a separate scoped request with evidence.

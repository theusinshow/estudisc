# 24 — Data Retention and Privacy

## Private profiles and AI

Student learning state belongs to its distinct owner; ADMIN access is limited to necessary support/QA summaries and authorized functions. Minimize contextual tutor data; exclude unrelated history. Tutor is optional and disabled in EXAM. Published approved content remains canonical. Historical attempts/evidence and referenced versions are retained through content retirement. All provider calls pass through the gateway.

## Principle

Collect and retain only what supports the learning experience, reliability or explicit user-requested history.

## User data

Attempts, notes, mistakes, projects, progress and exports may contain personal or proprietary information. They are private by default.

## Retention

Learning history is retained until the owner deletes it or restores a backup. Temporary execution runs may have shorter retention than official Attempts. Operational logs use limited retention and exclude raw learning content where possible.

## Export and deletion

The owner can export their content/state and request deletion. Deletion must explain whether backups or logs retain limited residual data and for how long once production infrastructure exists.

## External AI

No learning data is automatically sent to an AI provider. Teacher-context export is explicit and user-controlled. Any future integrated AI feature requires disclosure, data minimization and an ADR.

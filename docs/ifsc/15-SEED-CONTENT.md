# 15 — Seed Content Strategy

Status: **Accepted**
Source of truth for: initial content-production order.

## Principle

Engineering and content run in parallel. Do not wait for every platform feature before the student can study.

## Tier 0 — Golden content

Create/review at the highest quality:

- MAT-07 Percentage
- POR-01 Comprehension and inference
- CIE-06 Atomic structure
- GH-06 Slavery, resistance and abolition

These validate the renderer across quantitative, textual, diagrammatic and timeline/source-analysis patterns.

## Tier 1 — high centrality

Prioritize concepts that unlock many others or occur broadly in problem solving.

Examples:

### Mathematics
fractions, proportion, rule of three, percentage, first-degree equations, area/perimeter, charts.

### Portuguese
comprehension, inference, genres, argumentation, semantics, figures of speech.

### Science
matter, atoms, cells, ecology, body/health, energy.

### Geography/History
Santa Catarina, Colonial Brazil, slavery/resistance, Republic, globalization, World Wars.

## Tier 2 — complete the edital

All remaining official requirements are produced and validated. Historical absence never removes an official topic.

## Question volume

Avoid an artificial target such as 20 questions for every tiny Concept.

Initial aggregate target for a useful private system:

- first usable phase: ~200–300 validated questions;
- fuller bank: ~600–900 generated/curated questions plus official historical questions.

Quality and diversity are more important than near-duplicate volume.

## Duplicate control

Use normalized text/structure + Concept set initially. Add embedding similarity later only if needed.

## Content before CMS

Validated seed JSON is allowed before the advanced CMS exists. CMS later edits the same structured contracts.

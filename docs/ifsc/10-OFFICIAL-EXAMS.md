# 10 — Official Exam Preservation

Status: **Accepted**
Source of truth for: official question integrity and exposure.

## Imported editions

Primary Integrated set:

- IFSC Integrated 2025.1
- IFSC Integrated 2025.2
- IFSC Integrated 2026.1
- IFSC Integrated 2026.2

## Preservation rule

Official question wording, alternatives, assets, answer key and status are immutable historical content.

Corrections in the product are metadata corrections or a new representation version; never rewrite an official question to improve pedagogy.

## Allocation

- 2025.1: training/analysis
- 2025.2: training/analysis/mini assessments
- 2026.1: reserved intermediate full benchmark
- 2026.2: reserved final benchmark

2026.1 remains hidden from normal training until its scheduled benchmark. After completion, its questions may be released to review according to policy.

2026.2 remains hidden until the final benchmark and is never used earlier as training.

## Annulled item

2025.1 Q15:

- status `ANNULLED`;
- no score;
- no mastery evidence;
- retained for historical audit.

## Derived questions

Practice items inspired by an official structure are `DERIVED`, carry `derivedFromQuestionId`, and must use new wording/context where required. They are not official.

## Exposure integrity

Question selection checks per-student exposure before assignment. Repeated exposure reduces benchmark value and is visible in analytics.

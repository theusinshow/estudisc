# 00 — IFSC Product

Status: **Accepted**
Source of truth for: IFSC product goals and roles.

## Objective

Prepare a student for the IFSC 2027/1 Technical Integrated Courses entrance exam through an adaptive learning system rather than a passive digital handout.

The product loop is:

`teach → attempt → observe → feedback → evidence → review → next study decision`

The official exam is on 29 November 2026, with 28 multiple-choice questions, seven per area and four hours total.

## Roles

### ADMIN

Private content/system administrator. Can manage:

- curriculum mappings;
- lesson/question drafts;
- sources;
- QA;
- official exams;
- publication;
- analytics required for content health.

### STUDENT

Own learning state. Can:

- study;
- practice;
- review;
- take diagnostics and simulations;
- view progress;
- use the contextual tutor where permitted.

No public registration, classroom SaaS, marketplace, billing or public rankings are part of this expansion.

## Primary student job

The first screen must answer: **“What should I study now?”**

Primary path:

`Today → Start study → StudySession`

Manual curriculum exploration remains available but is not the default planning burden placed on the learner.

## Product modes

- `LEARN`
- `PRACTICE`
- `REVIEW`
- `EXAM`
- `BUILD` remains a programming/project extension in the core.

## Success model

Primary learning indicators:

- curriculum coverage;
- concept mastery;
- retention/review state;
- assessment performance;
- repeated error patterns;
- transfer to IFSC-style questions.

The system does **not** estimate probability of admission.

## Mobile priority

Student experience is mobile-first. Tablet and desktop remain first-class, but new student components are designed and acceptance-tested on mobile first. Programming Lab may progressively enhance for desktop width.

## Pedagogical frame

Abolition is treated as a historical process involving resistance and political/social action, not a single isolated date.

## Required flow

1. opening prediction about abolition;
2. short source/context;
3. identify forms of resistance;
4. source-analysis evidence selection;
5. timeline/order activity;
6. cause/effect activity;
7. abolition-process explanation;
8. independent source question;
9. IFSC-style transfer;
10. exit ticket.

## Required interactions

- ordering/timeline;
- source text highlight;
- classification/cause-effect.

Mobile timeline should recompose vertically or use an equivalent ordered-card interaction.

## Key contrast

`legal end of slavery` is not equivalent to `immediate end of social inequality`.

## Regression purpose

Validates timeline/order, source analysis, causal reasoning, historical evidence and mixed text/interaction mobile layout.

# Golden Lesson — POR-01 Comprehension and Inference

Status: **Regression fixture specification**
Kind: `CORE`
Target: 30–35 min

## Core pedagogical contrast

`possible` is not the same as `supported by the text`.

## Required flow

1. short text;
2. explicit-information check;
3. inference question;
4. text-highlight evidence selection;
5. worked reasoning;
6. guided analysis;
7. new text;
8. independent analysis;
9. IFSC-style transfer;
10. exit ticket.

## Example micro-text

A short scenario may imply rain through an umbrella/wet coat without explicitly stating “it was raining.” The lesson first asks whether rain was directly stated, then whether it is a supported inference.

## Required interaction

`text-highlight`

The learner selects the evidence span supporting an interpretation. On mobile, selection UX must not depend on precise desktop mouse dragging only; provide robust touch selection or sentence/segment selection.

## Distractor quality

Include plausible but unsupported statements to teach evidence discipline.

## Regression purpose

Validates rich text, selection/highlight, evidence-based feedback, long-form mobile reading and text-centered question layout.

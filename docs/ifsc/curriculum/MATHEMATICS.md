# Mathematics Curriculum — IFSC 2027

Status: **Accepted draft for seeding**
Official basis: Edital 05/DEING/2027/1, Anexo V, Matemática.

The Lesson IDs below are editorial groupings. Concepts are the atomic mastery targets.

## MAT-01 — Numbers and operations

Concepts:

- `MAT.NUM.NATURAL.INTEGER` — distinguish/use natural and integer numbers.
- `MAT.NUM.OPERATIONS` — perform and interpret the four operations.
- `MAT.NUM.ORDER` — compare/order signed numbers.
- `MAT.NUM.REAL.PROPERTIES` — apply properties of real-number operations.
- `MAT.NUM.EXPRESSIONS` — simplify/evaluate numeric expressions.

## MAT-02 — Divisibility, primes, MMC and MDC

- `MAT.DIV.DIVISIBILITY` — apply divisibility criteria.
- `MAT.DIV.PRIME` — identify prime/composite numbers.
- `MAT.DIV.FACTORIZATION` — decompose into prime factors.
- `MAT.DIV.MMC` — calculate/use least common multiple.
- `MAT.DIV.MDC` — calculate/use greatest common divisor.
- `MAT.DIV.MODEL` — decide whether MMC or MDC fits a contextual problem.

## Question-design rule

Most Portuguese assessment items should include authentic or purpose-built text stimuli. Isolated grammar drills may be used for acquisition/remediation but should not dominate transfer practice.

## Requirement coverage checklist

Final mapping must cover:

- comprehension of different discourse/text genres and modalities;
- written-language conventions;
- fone/fonema/grafema relations;
- morphology and syntax;
- inflections;
- active/passive voice/order;
- regency and agreement;
- logical-discursive operators;
- synonymy;
- ambiguity;
- figures of speech;
- argumentation;
- linguistic-literary resources/effects;
- cohesion/coherence;
- intertextuality;
- intentionality/context/authorship;
- Brazilian Portuguese varieties;
- phonological/lexical/syntactic differences;
- semantic effects;
- prestigious/stigmatized varieties;
- linguistic prejudice.
